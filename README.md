Pak Dedi's pantry printer — jammed since March — eats my boarding pass,
blinks PAPER JAM in cheerful green, and then, with enormous confidence,
prints a blank page. I thank it, apologise to it, photograph the blank page,
email it to my boss, and caption it: my printer's resignation letter.
